## ICRA 2024 in Yokohama, Japan!

At last month’s #ICRA2024 in Yokohama, Japan, I had an amazing opportunity to pull together all the work I have done throughout my PhD and present as an invited speaker at the 1st Workshop on Wearable Intelligence for Healthcare Robotics (WIHR): from Brain Activity to Body Movement.
 
I'm thrilled to share that I won the Best Presentation Award for my talk titled “Feeling the Future: Augmenting Medicine with Relocated Haptic Feedback in Extended Reality Environments”! 🎉
 
It was an incredible experience presenting at the WIHR workshop, and I'm deeply honored to have my work recognized among so many talented professionals. The certificate just arrived, and I couldn't wait to share this achievement with my network.
 
A huge thank you to the organizers, attendees, and everyone who supported me throughout this journey. This recognition motivates me even more to continue striving for excellence and contributing to our field. 🤖
