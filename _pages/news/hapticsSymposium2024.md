## 🌟🌐 Exciting Update from the IEEE World Haptics 2023 Conference! 🌐🌟

I am thrilled to share that I had the incredible opportunity to present my work at the World Haptics Conference in Delft, Netherlands. It was an amazing experience where I had several opportunities to showcase a demo and present a poster highlighting our work: "Haptic Feedback Relocation in Virtual Reality with Soft Haptic Displays." 

The conference was buzzing with cutting-edge research and innovation in the field of haptics, and I was honored to be a part of it. One of the highlights was participating in a panel discussion on Multisensory Cutaneous Displays, where we delved into the exciting possibilities of enhancing user experiences in virtual environments through tactile feedback. 

While I'm a few weeks late in sharing this update (oops!), I want to express my gratitude to everyone who made this experience possible – from the organizers to my fellow researchers and attendees. Special thanks to my advisor Allison Okamura and my collaborators at CHARM Lab Zhenish Zhakypov and Brian Vuong. Your passion and dedication to advancing haptic technology are truly inspiring.

Looking forward to continuing the dialogue and collaboration in this dynamic field. Stay tuned for more updates on my haptics, PhD journey, and beyond! 🤖
